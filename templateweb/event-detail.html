CivTech Hack 2026 — 48h building for local impact

July 5–6, 2026 Sat 9:00 AM – Sun 6:00 PM Orange Digital Center, Abidjan
event cover image — 1200×525

About the event

CivTech Hack is our flagship weekend hackathon: 48 hours, teams of up to four, one mission — build something that genuinely improves life in Côte d'Ivoire. Whether it's civic services, health, education, or local commerce, if it helps your community, it belongs here.

You'll get mentorship from senior engineers across the diaspora, free meals and workspace at the Orange Digital Center, and a demo day in front of partners and recruiters. No experience required — beginners team up with mentors.
