Senior Laravel Engineer

NEW
PayBox CI Abidjan, Plateau Remote OK Posted 2 days ago

About the role

PayBox CI is the mobile money aggregator powering payments for over half a million Ivorians. We're looking for a senior Laravel engineer to lead the backend of our core platform — someone who cares about clean architecture, financial correctness, and mentoring the next generation of Ivorian developers.

What you'll do

  • Own the architecture of our payments backend across Wave, Orange Money and MTN MoMo.
  • Design resilient, idempotent transaction flows that survive flaky networks.
  • Mentor mid-level and junior engineers through code review and pairing.
  • Drive our move to a multi-tenant model as we onboard new merchants.
  • Champion testing — we ship with Pest and we mean it.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years building production PHP, with at least 3 in Laravel.
  • Deep understanding of queues, caching and database design.
  • Experience with payments or other high-stakes domains is a big plus.
  • Clear written communication — we work partly async.
  • Fluency in French; English is a plus.

Why PayBox CI

  • Real impact — your code moves money for hundreds of thousands of people daily.
  • Hybrid: office in Plateau, remote when you need it.
  • Learning budget, conference tickets, and dedicated mentorship time.
